From Earth to the edge of the observable universe, in one continuous zoom, built on 109,400 catalogued stars and 715,000 catalogued galaxies. Drag to turn it, scroll or pinch to change scale.

How to read the colours

  • Real catalogue objects: stars, star clusters and galaxies at their measured positions. Bluer points are younger, redder ones older.
  • Our own address, from the Sun up to Laniakea
  • Sketches of mapped structures, drawn from published sizes where no catalogue was available here
  • Inferred from sparse bursts or quasars, and disputed
  • Voids, drawn as hollow outlines

Real galaxies cover only the patches of sky their surveys observed, so the empty directions are unobserved, not empty. Turn off "Sketches" to see the measured data alone. Each card says what is real and what is drawn. Distances beyond about a billion light-years are present-day (comoving) distances.
